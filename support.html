Support

Help with Madame Calista: Tarot Reading, purchases, and readings.

Contact

Open a support request. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if the problem is technical.

Common questions

I bought Calista Premium and the spreads are still locked.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in with the same one.

How do I cancel?
Open Settings in the app and tap Manage subscription, or go to your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Did my free reading reset?
Your free three-card reading comes back each day at midnight, your local time.

How do I turn off Calista's voice?
Open Settings in the app and switch off Calista's voice. She also stays quiet when your iPhone's silent switch is on, and a tap anywhere skips the line she is saying. The full text is always on screen.

Why does she only read some spreads aloud?
Calista reads your daily card aloud for everyone. In the other spreads, her voice is part of Calista Premium. The full reading is always on screen either way.

Where are my saved readings?
In the Journal, on your device only. They are not backed up to any server. An iPhone backup includes them.

The cards do not move.
Card motion is a switch in the app's Settings. If it is on and the cards still do not move, restart the app.

Can Madame Calista predict my future?
No. Madame Calista is for entertainment and reflection. The cards suggest; they do not decide. For medical, legal, financial, or mental-health questions, talk to a qualified person.

I am in crisis.
If you are in the United States, call or text 988. Anywhere else, contact your local emergency number.
