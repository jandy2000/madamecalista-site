Privacy Policy

Madame Calista: Tarot Reading for iPhone. Last updated October 10, 2026.

The short version: Madame Calista does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours.

What stays on your phone

  • Your questions and readings. Every reading is put together on your device. The questions you type are never sent anywhere.
  • Your journal. Saved readings and notes are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Your settings. Preferences such as reversals, card motion, sound and Calista's voice are stored on your device.

Calista's voice

Madame Calista's spoken lines are recorded in advance and built into the app. Nothing you type is spoken, sent or recorded. Her voice plays from the app itself, follows your silent switch, and can be turned off in Settings.

Purchases

Calista Premium (a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

On-device intelligence

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the app may use Apple's on-device language model to rephrase Madame Calista's lines. This runs entirely on your phone. Nothing is transmitted.

Crisis resources

If a question suggests you may be in crisis, the app pauses and shows crisis resources such as the 988 Lifeline. Tapping a resource opens your phone's dialer, messages, or browser. The app records nothing about it.

Children

Madame Calista does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a support request.

Madame Calista is an AI-generated character. The app is for entertainment and reflection. It does not predict the future and is not medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice.
